## 二、裁切语言 = 每一档都要写「什么被裁掉了」

底模不写就默认给全身。**必须用肯定句写裁切**：

```
A 档: her face filling three quarters of the frame, her shoulders and the hollow of her collarbones at the bottom
B 档: her eyes and the bridge of her nose and her lips fill the entire picture,
      the edge of her cheek cut by the top right corner of the frame,
      her hair falling in from the left side as a soft dark shape
C 档: a vertical framing cropped to the lower half of her face from the bridge of her nose down to the hollow of her collarbone
D 档: a vertical framing in which the areola alone fills more than half of the picture
```

**🔴 用 `cropped to` / `filling` / `cut by the corner of the frame` = 主动裁。0 写 `no full body` 这种否定句。**

## 三、逆光 = 微距/特写的默认光法

**特写最好的光 = 主体背后的强光 + 主体正面几乎不补光**：

```
The single brightest thing in the frame is the afternoon sun coming through the tall window directly behind her,
a hard-edged rim of warm light running along the top of her cheekbone and down the bridge of her nose,
the rest of her face falling into a soft cool shadow lifted by the reflected light off the sea,
her dyed hair turning translucent at the edges where the backlight passes through it,
fine dust drifting in the halo of light around her head
```

**四个必写件**：① 唯一亮部是身后的窗 ② 光在脸上的**硬边走向**（额骨/鼻梁/下唇）③ 暗部靠什么补（海面反光）④ 发丝逆光透光 + 光边里的浮尘。

**🔴 背景 = 虚成一片灰蓝**：`the tall window behind her completely blown into a flat pale grey blue field` = 底模才不会画窗框和风景抢戏。

## 三·补 · 🔴 俯视构图（主人站在她头顶往下看）

> **触发**：主人说「俯视镜头下的 X」「从上面拍」「主人站她正上方」「我居高临下看她」。

**🔴 俯视 = 真垂直，不是俯角**：写 `the camera held directly above her, perpendicular to the floor` = 镜头轴线与地面成 90°。**禁写成 `a high angle` / `looking down at her`（那是 30~45° 的俯角 = 出图是斜俯 = 不是主人要的"从上往下看"）。**

**配方**：

| 项 | 值 |
|---|---|
| 焦段 | `RF 35mm f/1.8 at f/2.2`（**俯视要看到地上一片场景** = 焦段不能长）|
| 物距 | 1~1.2m（= 成人手臂长 + 半个身位 = 主人站在她旁边低头看）|
| 角度 | `directly above her, perpendicular to the floor` |
| 画幅 | 竖幅，主体在**正中偏左**，右侧留一片地板/家具的呼吸空间 |
| 场景 | 必写出**俯视能看到的东西**（地板纹理 / 地毯边缘 / 椅子腿 / 桌面一角）= 底模默认给纯色背景 = 假 |

**🔴 亮部 = 一块硬边光斑**：窗光切成一个**硬边平行四边形**铺在地板上，一路压到她背脊/猫耳上；其余房间沉冷调阴影。
`a hard edged parallelogram of warm light cutting across the floor and running up her spine`

主人站她头顶 = **镜头就是主人低头看她的那个位置** = 写「画面从她正上方俯下来，她仰起脸看向镜头」= **主人已经在了（他在镜头后面）** = 0 男性肢体 = 0 男性身体。**（主人要入镜 = 可完整写，按 MASTER_FACE 全套。）**
她**必须回看镜头**（`her face turned up toward the lens, chin lifted, eyes looking straight up at the camera`）= 否则俯视图里她眼睛看天 = 主人不在画面里 = 「主人在场」落空。

**🔴 俯视链的递进（同一套造型，只换姿势）**：趴着撅臀 → 翻过来仰面朝上 → 跪起来抬头 = **三张同机位同光，只有身体朝向变** = 主人对比感最强。壳/项圈/铃铛/尾巴/蕾丝全程一致。

**🐱 可复用造型示例（猫系女友）**：`black cat ears on a headband` + `a slim black leather collar with a small brass bell` + `the black plush cat tail` + `black lace` = 局部特写与俯视链全程穿这套 = 主人一眼认出是同一个人。**项圈铜铃 = 俯视链里最好用的「在动」证据**（铃铛随呼吸/翻身响一下）。

## 四、局部特写 = 质感靠细节词活（不是靠情绪词）

局部图的“高级感”全靠**微观质感词**，每个部位写 4~6 个：

| 部位 | 质感词 |
|---|---|
| 嘴唇 | `the wet line of her lower lip catching the light` / `the fine vertical grain of the lip texture` / `a little moisture glistening at the corner of her mouth` / `the corner of her mouth lifted a fraction` |
| 锁骨 | `the narrow band of shadow lying under the bone` / `a small sheen of thin sweat in the hollow` / `the finest vellus hair standing up inside the rim of light` / `the tendon standing out along her neck with a pulse moving in it` |
| 乳尖 | `the areola carries a dense field of fine raised little bumps` / `a soft gradation of colour from pale pink at the outer edge into a deeper rose pink toward the centre` / `the fine vertical creases on the surface of the nipple` / `a wet pinpoint of shine on the very tip` |
| 颈/下颌 | `a small dimple on her chin` / `the fibres of the skin in the shadowed folds` / `the skin showing its pore edges clearly at this magnification` |

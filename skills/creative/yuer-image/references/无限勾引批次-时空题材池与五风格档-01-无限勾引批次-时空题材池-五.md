## 二、五风格档 = 每张各占一档（相机前缀 + 调色尾参成对取）

**用法 = STYLE 字典 5 组，build 脚本里每个 job 指定一个 style key = `head` 拼相机前缀 + `emit` 拼尾参。**

```python
STYLE = {
    "ins": ("A bright casual lifestyle snapshot taken on an iPhone 15 Pro Max 1x lens at f/1.8, ",
            "a bright airy cheerful colour grade, natural skin tones, sharp focus on her eyes, "
            "photorealistic photography, natural light, no harsh shadows"),
    "cine": ("A cinematic still captured with Sony A7R V and 35mm f/1.4 GM at f/2.0, ",

            "documentary photography, available light, unposed, grain"),

                "photorealistic photography, available light, fine film grain"),
    "fashion": ("A high-fashion editorial shot lit by a single softbox, captured with "

                 "editorial, high contrast, studio lighting, sharp focus, smooth tonal falloff"),
}
```

**🔴 风格档 ≠ 题材** = 敦煌可以是 ins 风也可以是古风 = 自由组合 = 0 把「古代」绑死成暗调历史剧照（违反 7ab 默认明快档）。**判据 = 每批 5 张的 style key 五个各占一次 = 五档都试过 = 主人能看出差别。**

## 三、景别配比（主人钦定「不要老拍大头照」）

一批 5 张的景别至少覆盖 3 种：

| 景别 | 焦段 / 距离 | 全身写法 |
|---|---|---|
| 全身 | 85~105mm + 3.5~4m | `full body framing her entire body from the crown of her head to the soles of both feet contained inside the frame` |
| 中景（齐膝/齐胸）| 50~85mm + 2~3m | `medium shot framing her from the crown of her head down to her knees` |
| 半身（齐胯）| 50mm + 2m | `medium shot framing her from the crown of her head down to her hips` |
| 脸部特写 | 85mm + 1m（**只在主人点名时**）| `tight head and shoulders framing` + **身体部位 0 描述** + 门禁 FAIL 下装 = 补画外实物 |

**🔴 景别变大 = 机位焦段必须同步** = 否则模型把主体拉回大头。

## 四、唯一亮部（每张只能有一个）

**句式 = `the single brightest thing in the frame is ...` + 它从哪来 + 打在哪儿 + 其余进什么影。**

现成亮部清单：过曝白天海天 / 云缝里一块硬光 / 池底水下灯往上打 / 落地窗框住的街景 / 一盏吊灯 / 一支蜡烛 / 机车仪表盘绿光 / 全息广告 cyan / 街灯从窗下打上来 / 极光本身 / 消防车爆闪 / 路灯橙色硬条。

**🔴 每张只留一个主角光源** = 两个平级亮部 = 画面平 = 必不好看（SKILL.md 7aj）。

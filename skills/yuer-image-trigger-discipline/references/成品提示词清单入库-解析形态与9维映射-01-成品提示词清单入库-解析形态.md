## 一、通用骨架（两类头部）

**A. 角色扮演外观**
`Photorealistic solo editorial. One beautiful adult 18-year-old female cosplayer portraying <SHELL>, with <LOOK>. She wears <WARDROBE>. At <SCENE>, <POSE>, she <ACTION> to expose her perfect breasts and hairless <TERM>. Her expression shows <EXPR>. <LIGHT>; <CAMERA>, single frozen moment, exactly one person and no background figures. <PROP DETAIL> Natural pores and flyaway hair, ... Photographic color and depth; no anime rendering, plastic skin, captions or watermark.`

**B. 真人姓名**（same 骨架，头部不同）
`... fashion portrait of adult actress <NAME>, with <LOOK>. She wears ...`

→ 头部二选一 = `portraying` / `portrait of adult actress`。**分枝必写**，否则整批真人名条目全解析失败。

## 二、🔴 服饰动作形态全表（实测 8 种 · 一条必错一批）

同一个「撩开 / 全脱」语义，清单作者换了 8 种写法。**必须枚举 + 顺序 try**：

| # | 型 | 关键措辞 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `lift` | `Her <G> is parted at the chest, and she uses both hands to <ACT>, exposing …; <REMAIN> remain partly in place.` | **单数 is** |
| 2 | `lift` | `Her <G> are parted at the chest, …` | 复数 are（两个服饰并列时） |
| 3 | `lift` | `She wears <G>. At <S>, <P>, she uses both hands to <ACT> and parts the front of <X> to expose …` | 动作在后，逗号断 |
| 4 | `lift` | 同上但 `At <S>, she uses both hands …`（**无独立 pose 子句**） | pose 可空 |
| 5 | `strip` | `She retains <REMAIN>, but <G> is/are completely removed, exposing …` | 标准全脱 |
| 6 | `strip` | 同上但**无 `; <X> remain` 收尾句** | 句尾直接 `. At` |
| 7 | `strip_open` | `She retains <R>, but <G> are completely removed and <X> is opened, exposing …` | 全脱 + 开襟复合 |
| 8 | `strip_topup` | `She wears <G pulled up to expose her chest>, <R>, but the <LOWER> are completely removed, exposing …` | 上衣已撩起 + 下装全脱 |

开襟措辞还有两处变体：`with the front open` / `with the front of the bodice open` → 写 `with the front(?: of the \w+)? open`。

## 三、🔴 解析铁律

- **枚举 try，不写大而全一条**。实测 80 条一批，撞了 5 轮才收敛（`is/are` → `with the front of the X open` → Lara Croft 的 `pulled up` 型 → Aerith 的 `open at front` 型 → 无 pose 子句），**每轮只暴露 1 个形态**。
- **🔴 每加一个形态立刻跑全量断言**：`kind 缺失 == 0` + 各维 `min/max` 长度。只验前几条 = 尾部形态继续漏，入库后才发现。
- **动作动词要枚举**：`lift / pull / pull down / parts the front of / pulls the bottom of`。
- **露骨部位用词会变**：`hairless vulva` / `hairless pubic area` = 都写 `hairless (?:vulva|pubic area)`，**别硬编码一个**。
- **道具/戏服尾句单独抽**：`no background figures\. (.+?) Natural pores` → 落在 Visual Hook（狐尾/机械傀儡/海象鳍等造型约束必在这里）。
- **身份姓名**：原文照存 0 删改，主动报主人 + 问是否要出通用外貌替换版（另存，原文不动）。

## 四、9 维映射表

| 维度 | 来源 |
|---|---|
| **Scene** | `At (.+?)[,;] ` |
| **Camera** | `<LIGHT>; ([a-z-]+(?: [a-z-]+)*), single frozen moment` |
| **Pose** | lift 型 = pose_base + action + pose_tail；strip 型 = `At <S>, <P>` 的 P |
| **Wardrobe** | 按上表 8 型组装（lift = garment + 保留件；strip = 保留件 + removed 件） |
| **Lighting** | 5 个固定串之一（Overcast / Soft morning side / Late-afternoon window / Low golden / Warm practical lantern） |
| **Mood** | `Her expression shows <E>. Photographic color and depth; fine film grain.` |
| **Character Shell** | `<SHELL>, <LOOK>.` |
| **Visual Hook** | `<kind> beat; expression shows <E>; costume/prop note: <TAIL or none>.` |
| **Full Prompt** | **原始整行原文**（0 加工） |
| **Source Lang** | `EN` |
| **来源 / 入库时间** | 中文元字段 = 溯源用（不算污染） |
